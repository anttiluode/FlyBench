How to play

Start the synthetic room. Patch A flickers fast; patch B flickers slowly. When your chosen target is bright, press teach now, hit Space, or click the ecology. Do it repeatedly. Matching flies accumulate eligibility, receive more food, anchor, and bud. Use reverse target and teach the other patch; the tuning histogram can move back.

Camera mode replaces the synthetic pixels with your webcam. Nothing is uploaded: the video stays in the browser and is sampled locally by the flies.

The actual rules

z ← r·e^(iω)·z + (1−r)·d
eligibility decays from recent contribution; a click distributes a bounded food budget through that eligibility; high-energy flies bud with a small tuning mutation. Rewarded fly positions deposit growth resource. No click → no reward → no arbor resource.

This is a public visual port of the FlyBench mechanism, not a claim that a fly colony is a biological neuron. The Python bench remains the measured reference.
